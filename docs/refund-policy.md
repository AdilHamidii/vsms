# Refund Policy — vSIM OTP

_Last updated: 30 May 2026_

## Automatic refunds (in credits)

A credit is **automatically returned to your in-app balance** when:

- No verification code arrives within the 20-minute reservation window.
- The number is canceled before any code is received (tap the X on the
  Waiting screen).
- The upstream telephony provider returns an error before the number is
  assigned.

These refunds are issued in credits, not cash. They appear in your
balance within seconds of the failure being detected.

## No refunds when

- The verification code was successfully delivered to the assigned
  number — the service was rendered, regardless of whether you used
  the code on the destination platform.
- You used the number for purposes prohibited by our Terms of Use.

## App Store purchase refunds (real money)

In-App Purchases for credit packs are processed by Apple. To request a
refund for a credit pack purchase, use Apple's official refund flow:
**https://reportaproblem.apple.com**. We do not handle Apple purchase
refunds directly.

If Apple grants your refund, the corresponding credits may be deducted
from your wallet.

## Questions

Email **support@example.com** with your order ID and a brief description
of the issue, and we'll respond within 2 business days.
