## Description (4000 chars)

```
vSIM OTP gives you a clean, calm iOS app for renting temporary phone numbers and receiving SMS verification codes.

Protect your real number from spam and breaches. Get a number for any service that accepts SMS verification, receive the code in seconds, and move on.

WHAT IT DOES
• Pick a country and service from a curated list
• Tap once to rent a fresh number
• Receive your SMS automatically — copy with one tap
• See a live count of how long is left before the number expires
• Auto-refund if no code arrives within 20 minutes

HOW IT WORKS
• Sign in with Apple — no email or password forms
• Get 5 free credits to try the app
• Buy more credits with one tap when you need them
• Every credit pack is a one-time purchase. Credits never expire.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
• Quiet, Apple-utility design that respects your time
• No ads. No tracking. No upsells.
• Push notifications when your code arrives — even if the app is in the background
• Honest pricing: the credit pack price is the price. No hidden fees.
• Transparent refund policy: if the system fails, you keep your credit.

WHO IT'S FOR
vSIM OTP is for people who want a second phone number for short-term, lawful purposes — protecting privacy when trying a new service, testing your own product, or recovering account access. It is not a tool for fraud or for bypassing legitimate identity checks. Please use only on services where temporary numbers are permitted by their terms.

PRICING
• 5 credits — $2.99
• 12 credits — $5.99 (best value per credit)
• 30 credits — $12.99

CREDITS
1 credit = 1 standard verification. Premium routes may cost 2 credits per code.

QUESTIONS
Email support@example.com. Privacy policy and terms inside the app under Account → Legal.
```
