# Privacy Policy — vSIM OTP

_Last updated: 30 May 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains what data **vSIM OTP** ("we", "us") collects
when you use the iOS application of the same name, how we use it, and the
choices you have. Contact: **adil.hamidii123@gmail.com**.

## What we collect

When you sign in and use the app, we receive and store:

- **Apple account identifier and email address** — from Sign in with Apple.
  We use this only to identify your account and contact you about your
  account. We do not use it for marketing.
- **Wallet balance and transaction history** — credits you purchase and
  spend within the app.
- **Order history** — for each temporary number you request: the service
  and country you chose, the assigned phone number, any verification code
  received, the time and outcome.
- **Push notification token** — a device identifier issued by Apple
  Push Notification service, used solely to deliver notifications when a
  verification code arrives for an order you placed.
- **Purchase records** — the App Store transaction identifier and product
  identifier for any in-app purchase you make, used to credit your wallet
  and detect duplicates.
- **Crash and diagnostic logs** — aggregated, non-identifiable performance
  data sent to Apple by your device.

We do **not** collect or store: your contacts, location, photos,
microphone audio, advertising identifier, browsing history, or your real
phone number.

## How we use it

- To provide the core service: request a temporary phone number from our
  upstream provider on your behalf, deliver the verification code to you,
  and refund credits when delivery fails.
- To process purchases through the Apple App Store and grant the
  corresponding credits.
- To send push notifications about the orders you place.
- To detect and prevent fraud and abuse.
- To comply with legal obligations.

We do **not** sell or rent your personal data to anyone. We do **not** use
your data for advertising or tracking. We do not share your information
with third parties except as required to provide the service:

- **Supabase** (database + auth + edge functions) — hosts your account
  and order records.
- **SMSPVA** (telephony provider) — receives the country and service you
  selected so it can assign and route a temporary number to your account.
  SMSPVA does not receive your identity or contact details.
- **Apple** — App Store purchase verification and APNs delivery.

## Storage and retention

- Account, wallet, and order records are stored as long as you have an
  active account.
- Push notification tokens are kept until you sign out, delete the app,
  or revoke notification permission.
- You can delete your account and all associated data from within the
  app at any time: **Account → Danger zone → Delete account.** Deletion
  is permanent and cannot be reversed. Order history older than 30 days
  is automatically purged.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we
hold about you. Email **adil.hamidii123@gmail.com** with your request, or use
in-app account deletion. We respond within 30 days.

## International transfers

Data is stored in the Supabase region you were assigned when you signed
up. Push notifications transit through Apple servers worldwide.

## Children

vSIM OTP is rated 17+ and is not intended for children under 17.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will change when
we do. Material changes are surfaced in the app on next launch.
