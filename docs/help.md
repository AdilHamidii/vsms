# Help Center — vSIM OTP

## How does vSIM OTP work?

1. Sign in with Apple.
2. Pick a service (Messenger A, Banky, Foodbox, etc.) and a country.
3. Tap **Get number** — a credit is deducted from your balance and a
   real phone number is reserved for you.
4. Use the number on the target service to request a verification code.
5. The code arrives in vSIM OTP automatically — tap **Copy code** and
   paste it into the target service.

## How long is the number reserved?

20 minutes. If no code arrives in that window, the credit is refunded
automatically.

## My code isn't arriving — what can I do?

- Tap **Check now** on the Waiting screen to ping our provider directly.
- Make sure the target service actually sent the SMS — sometimes they
  silently drop messages from temporary numbers.
- If 20 minutes pass with no code, your credit is refunded and you can
  try a different service or country.

## How do I buy credits?

Account tab → **Top up**, or tap the credit pill on the Home screen.
Three pack sizes are available, billed through Apple In-App Purchase.

## How do I delete my account?

Account → Danger zone → **Delete account**. This permanently removes
your account, balance, and order history. Pending orders are
auto-canceled before deletion.

## I tapped Sign out but want to come back

Just open the app and sign back in with the same Apple ID. Your wallet
balance and order history are preserved.
